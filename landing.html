The Structural Challenge

Dementia care in NER faces a geography problem, not just a medical one.

Across the 8 states of North-Eastern India, terrain barriers, annual review blindspots, and Western test batteries isolate elderly cohorts from timely clinical oversight.

8–14 Hours Care Access Gap

Terrain & Transit Barrier

Reaching tertiary medical centers like GMCH Guwahati or NEIGRIHMS Shillong requires grueling multi-leg mountain transit over monsoon-damaged routes. Over 78% of suspected dementia cases in districts like Dimapur, Kokrajhar, and Gangtok never receive a secondary clinical follow-up.

365-Day Blindspot Systematic-Monitoring Gap

Infrequent Outpatient Reviews

Annual outpatient consultations cannot detect subtle, progressive cognitive decline. Without continuous home telemetry across 14-day rolling windows, progressive micro-losses in executive routine and visual reaction time remain unflagged until irreversible dependency occurs.

8 Native Dialects Language Battery Gap

Standardized Tests Fail in the Hills

Urban English and Hindi cognitive screening batteries produce false impairment markers among tribal elders. Assessment batteries must integrate indigenous rural idioms and everyday cultural landmarks across Assamese, Bodo, Khasi, Manipuri, Mizo, and Nepali.

AI flags with visible reasoning

  • Mathematical delta: Δ % ≤ -15% sustained drop triggers a critical neurologist alert.
  • Domain-isolated triage: Distinguishes cognitive decline from motor tremor artifacts.
  • Adherence cross-reference: Correlates gameplay dips against missed medication windows.

Multi-Tier Healthcare Coordination

Engineered for Hospital Neurologists and Grassroots Field Workers Alike

Bridging the clinical governance gap between state medical colleges and remote hamlet health sub-centers with role-tailored tooling.

Primary & Tertiary Care

For Clinicians

Longitudinal objectivity to evaluate cognitive drift and therapeutic efficacy between clinic appointments, without relying solely on 15-minute annual outpatient visits.

  • Systematic multi-patient roster: Cohort-wide stratification with rolling 14-day baseline drift detection.
  • Explainable AI flagging: Transparent statistical rationale with exposed mathematical thresholds (Δ ≤ -15%).
  • Exportable clinical reports: 5-domain composite cognitive engagement breakdown and 12-week longitudinal trendlines.
  • Doctor-only clinical notes: Private clinical record entries and customized pharmacological schedule management.
Community & Primary Health

For Health Workers

High-contrast, intuitive mobile tools enabling ASHA workers, Anganwadi coordinators, and visiting nurses to administer standardized care in the field.

  • Multi-patient cluster view: Patients organized by district, hamlet, and village sub-center for field logistics.
  • Offline sync for field visits: Full offline PWA operation in remote hill tracts with automated catch-up sync.
  • Adherence tracking across assigned patients: Silent adherence logging with zero-guilt, non-punitive caregiver prompts.
  • Elder-friendly guidance: 72px touch targets and native dialect audio instructions requiring minimal digital literacy.

Offline PWA & Zero Cloud Latency

Operates without internet connectivity during remote hill visits. Session logs, encrypted local audio, and reminder schedules cache locally in IndexedDB and synchronize automatically upon cellular reconnect.

Elderly-Accessible Hardware Standards

Runs on entry-level 7–10" Android tablets with high-contrast displays, physical volume rocker support, 72px minimum touch boundaries, and algorithmic tremor filtering to accommodate motor instability.
